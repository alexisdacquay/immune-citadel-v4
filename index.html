YOUR DATA

The game sends a few anonymous play stats to our own server: which level you play, how it ends, how long you read each card. No name, no save code, nothing that says who you are. They help us make levels fairer. They are deleted after 13 months.

Each time the game loads (only with play stats on), and if it crashes, a report goes to Sentry, a company that helps us find bugs and keeps the reports in the EU. A report has no name and no internet address in it. Like any website, Sentry's servers see your internet address while they talk to your browser, but Sentry is set never to keep it. Your cloud save stays on our server (Render, in Frankfurt) under your save code. Our server forgets your internet address within two minutes and never saves it.

The game makes your cloud save by itself. If it does not change for 13 months, it is deleted. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/alexisdacquay/immune-citadel-v4/issues (it needs a GitHub account; under 13, ask a parent or teacher to open it). We cannot promise when we will answer. Never post your save code there: it is the only key to your save.
